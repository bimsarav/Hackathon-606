# Glossary — airline knowledge base

Merged from the terminology tables of the research notes (regenerate after editing them). Source IDs resolve in `SOURCES.md`; the note column says where the term is discussed in context. A term defined in several notes appears once per note — differences are deliberate context, flag real conflicts in the notes' open questions.
