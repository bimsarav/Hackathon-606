# ADR-0006: Business-hours SLA computed in incident-service

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-19) · rules pending PO review
- **Context:** SLA clocks depend on office calendars, holidays, DST and pause periods (BQ-1, BQ-2).
- **Decision:** A pure SLA engine computes response and resolution due dates with paused minutes; the dates are stored on the incident. A scheduled scan flags breaches and escalates once per trigger.
- **Consequences:** Fast queries and reports. Calendar changes affect new or updated incidents only.
