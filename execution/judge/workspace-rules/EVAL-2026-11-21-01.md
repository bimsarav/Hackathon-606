## E. Risks and missing considerations
- **Inferred risk, cost/fatigue:** a score on every completion, plus a subagent each time, may push sessions to skip or rubber-stamp the step (J-WR-005, J-WR-006).
- **Inferred risk, same-model bias:** a fresh context reduces anchoring but not shared blind spots of the same model; no rule asks for human spot checks of Judge reports or calibration against the client's eventual judging.
- **Missing:** what happens when the Judge itself is wrong (dispute path: producer rebuttal recorded, user decides).
- **Missing:** a rule for timing pressure (e.g. hackathon deadline): whether a REWORK verdict may be overridden by the user for submission, recorded as accepted risk (J-WR-004).
- **Missing:** criteria versioning tie-in: reports should record the CRITERIA.md version/date they used, so scores stay comparable when criteria change.
- **Verified context:** disruption-advisor is close to submission with no baseline evaluation; the criteria file already predicts at least one mandatory-rule failure (13-slide deck vs 10-page limit, per RESUME.md T-628) and open reboot-test status (DEPLOYMENT_STATUS: OPERATIONAL VERIFICATION PENDING). A baseline full evaluation should run soon.
